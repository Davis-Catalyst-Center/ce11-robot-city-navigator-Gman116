Both A* and Dijkstra's were both effective algorithms at finding the quickest route between two nodes and greedy sometimes found the best route 
but not always. Dijkstra's would have had to look for longer to find the optimal path compared to A* but for such a small graph there was minimal
difference. For a larger graph (such as a city or country) in real life A* would be much preferable because it finds the shortest path faster.
In the case of the map given for this assignment greedy could always find a path, but it is not always certain that greedy will find a path.
Even when greedy does find a path it is often much slower then dijkstra and A* and would take longer to find. This would be even more noticable
for a larger graph. When it comes to small graphs it doesn't matter a lot whether you use dijkstra's or A* but for larger graphs A* is needed or
even for smaller graphs where quick solving time is needed a lot such as for a pathfinding algorithm for a npc in a video game.
